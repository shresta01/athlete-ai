# Runbook: Biomechanics & Training Split Safeguards
Signatures: "Bench Press", "Squats", "Deadlift", "Overhead Press"

### Training Volume Constraints
1. Progressive overload requires calculating total set volume: Volume = Weight * Reps.
2. If total weekly volume increases by more than 10-15%, flag an overtraining safety warning to prevent injury.
3. Ensure compound movements maintain strict form tips (e.g., foot placement, core bracing).
