## 2. Structural Split Blueprint Assembly
Assemble the weekly routine schedule cleanly based on the user's explicitly requested training frequency:

### A. Full-Body Routine Structure (Target: High Efficiency / Weight Loss)
*   **Frequency:** 3 days/week (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday). Alternating rest days are mandatory.
*   **Session Design:** Pick 1 Primary Compound Lift from Chest, 1 from Back, and 1 from Legs. Add 1 Core exercise at the end. Perform 3-4 working sets of 6-10 repetitions per exercise.

### B. Muscle Group Isolation Split (Target: Target Hypertrophy)
*   **Frequency:** 4-5 days/week.
*   **Session Design:** Group matching targets together (e.g., Push days = Chest/Shoulders/Triceps; Pull days = Back/Biceps). Pair 2 Compound Lifts with 2 Isolation Alternates per target muscle group.

### C. Cardio Architecture
*   **Low-Intensity Steady State (LISS):** Walking, cycling, or rowing at a sustained, easy pace. Best utilized during deep caloric deficits (Cutting) to maximize total energy expenditure while minimizing central nervous system exhaustion.
*   **High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT):** Sprints or stationary bike intervals (e.g., 30 seconds max effort, 60 seconds active recovery). Best utilized during athletic conditioning blocks or quick, time-restricted training routines.
